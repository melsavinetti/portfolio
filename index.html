About

I'm a design executive with a background spanning product strategy, research, and hands-on craft. I've led UX organizations at Series B startups and Fortune 500 companies alike — building the teams, systems, and culture that let design do its best work.

My approach is rooted in clarity: understanding what people actually need, what the business is trying to achieve, and finding the elegant overlap between the two. I believe the best design work happens when research and craft are inseparable.

When I'm not thinking about systems and strategy, I'm usually exploring type design, reading about cognitive psychology, or looking for the best espresso in whatever city I'm in.
